The brief

A daily Wordle-style puzzle for Dota players, played on the web and inside Discord: guess the six items a pro finished a big tournament match with. It should feel like an esports broadcast — sharp, competitive, a daily ritual you share with your server — without borrowing Valve's artwork.

competitivesharpdailysocial

Success: ownable outside the sword-and-shield gaming clichés, reads at Discord avatar size, ties to the name, and comes from the game itself.

Final design

Build D

Six item tiles assemble into a D: the build is the brand.

  • Six tiles = the six inventory slots every answer fills
  • Gold for Dota's currency, green for the slot you got right
  • A D on a 3×3 grid: square, bold, survives a Discord avatar
  • Wordmark in the game's own display face, converted to outlines
